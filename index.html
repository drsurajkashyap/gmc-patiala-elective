How counselling works

Every student follows the same merit-based queue. Your choices become active only when your merit rank reaches the front of the queue.

1

Login

Use your Roll Number as your User ID and the password issued to you.

2

Wait in the merit queue

See the active merit rank, students ahead and approximate waiting time.

3

Choose a timeslot

When your turn activates, choose either First Timeslot or Second Timeslot.

4

Select electives

Select exactly one Block A elective and one Block B elective within that timeslot.

5

Confirm

Review your selections carefully. Final confirmation completes your counselling.

Understand the two timeslots

A timeslot is a complete Block A + Block B posting combination. Both electives must belong to the same timeslot.

First Timeslot

Block A first · Block B second

A
First Posting Period Your selected Block A elective
B

Second Timeslot

Block B first · Block A second

B
First Posting Period Your selected Block B elective

Important: You cannot mix Block A from First Timeslot with Block B from Second Timeslot, or vice versa. Both electives must be available together in the same selected timeslot.

While you are waiting

Approximate Wait

🔎

When you are within 20 students of your turn, check whether your preferred Block A + B combination is currently available together.

Preference lookup does not reserve seats. Availability may change before your turn because students ahead of you may select the same electives.

What you should do

Keep the process simple: prepare before your turn, act carefully during your turn, and verify your final allocation afterward.

BEFORE YOUR TURN

Prepare your preferences

  • Keep your Roll Number and password ready.
  • Review multiple Block A and Block B options.
  • Use the live seat matrix to keep alternatives ready.
  • Keep a stable internet connection and sufficient battery.
DURING YOUR TURN

Choose carefully

  • Select one complete timeslot.
  • Select one Block A and one Block B elective.
  • Review both choices before final confirmation.
  • Do not refresh or repeatedly press Confirm while submission is processing.
AFTER CONFIRMATION

Verify your allocation

  • Your final Block A and Block B selections are shown on screen.
  • Your posting sequence is displayed according to the selected timeslot.
  • A successfully confirmed allocation is treated as final.
  • Keep the confirmation visible until you have verified all details.

Understand counselling status

The portal clearly shows whether counselling has not started, is currently running, or has been paused by the counselling team.

🟢 COUNSELLING RUNNING The merit queue is actively progressing. Keep monitoring your position and seat availability.
🟠 COUNSELLING PAUSED The current merit rank is preserved. Wait for the administrator to resume counselling.
⚪ COUNSELLING NOT STARTED You may log in and verify your details, but no student can make a final allocation yet.

Important counselling rules

These rules protect merit order, seat accuracy, and fairness throughout the counselling process.

1
Counselling proceeds strictly by merit Only the student whose merit rank is currently active can complete final counselling.
2
Choose one complete timeslot Your Block A and Block B electives must both belong to the same selected timeslot.
3
Seat availability is live Availability can change as students ahead of you complete counselling.
4
Preference lookup does not reserve seats It is only a planning aid to help you prepare your preferred combination and alternatives.
5
Draft selections do not reserve seats Seats are consumed only after successful final confirmation by the counselling system.
6
Final confirmation is final Check the selected timeslot, Block A elective, Block B elective and posting sequence before confirming.
7
Do not repeatedly submit After pressing final confirmation, wait for the system response. Do not repeatedly press the button or refresh the page while submission is processing.
8
Timer expiry does not automatically skip you If your counselling timer expires, wait for administrator intervention. Do not attempt to bypass the system.
9
Administrator intervention may occasionally be required In case of a technical or operational issue, the counselling administrator may take temporary control of the active student's counselling.
