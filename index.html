Guide to Electives Counselling

Please read the following information before participating in counselling.

1

Merit-Based Counselling

Counselling is conducted according to the approved merit rank. Students are invited to make their selections sequentially according to their rank.

2

Wait for Your Turn

Students should log in to the portal and wait for their merit rank to become active. The portal displays the current counselling status and available elective seats.

3

Select Block A Elective

When your turn becomes active, select one available elective from the Block A elective options.

4

Select Block B Elective

Select one available elective from the Block B elective options during your allotted counselling turn.

5

Check Seat Availability

Elective seats are limited. The available seat count shown on the portal is updated as students complete their allocations.

6

Confirm Your Selections

Carefully review both selected electives before final confirmation. Once confirmed, the allocation is recorded and cannot be changed.

7

Final Allocation

After successful confirmation, your elective allocation is completed and recorded in the counselling system.

8

Keep Your Login Credentials Secure

Use only your assigned login credentials. Do not share your username or password with any other person.

Important Instructions

  • Counselling follows the approved merit sequence.
  • Students should ensure a stable internet connection during their turn.
  • Elective availability may change as students preceding you complete their allocations.
  • Please review both selections carefully before final confirmation.
  • Once confirmed, elective allocations cannot be changed through the portal.
  • Students who miss or exceed their allotted counselling time may be handled according to the applicable counselling rules.
