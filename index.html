How counselling works

The process is intentionally simple. Please read these steps before your turn.

1

Login

Use your Roll Number as User ID and the password issued to you.

2

Wait for your turn

The portal shows your Hall, active Merit Rank, students ahead and live seat availability.

3

Select electives

When your turn becomes active, select exactly one Block A and one Block B elective.

4

Confirm once

Check both choices carefully and submit the final confirmation within your allotted time.

Student Instructions

Please follow these instructions to keep counselling fast and fair for everyone.

Keep your login credentials private. Do not share your password or allow another student to submit choices from your account.

Before counselling

  • Use a phone or computer with stable internet and sufficient battery.
  • Keep your Roll Number/User ID and password ready.
  • Review your preferred Block A and Block B electives in advance.
  • You may remain logged in while waiting for your merit turn.
  • Seat availability shown in the portal is live and changes as counselling progresses.

When your turn becomes active

  • You will receive a fixed counselling window shown by an exact countdown timer.
  • Select one Block A and one Block B elective.
  • Only electives with available seats can be successfully allotted.
  • Review both selections carefully before final confirmation.
  • Do not repeatedly press Confirm or refresh while submission is processing.

While you are waiting

  • Your Hall, Merit Rank, Roll Number and student details are displayed after login.
  • When counselling is running, you can see the current active Merit Rank, students ahead and an approximate wait time.
  • The approximate wait is indicative only and may change depending on earlier students.
  • Live Block A and Block B seat matrices remain visible so you can keep alternative choices ready.

After final confirmation

  • Your confirmed Block A and Block B allotments are shown on screen.
  • A successfully confirmed allotment is treated as final.
  • If an administrator takes control of your counselling, follow the message shown on screen.
  • For a technical problem during your active turn, immediately contact the counselling team at the venue.
